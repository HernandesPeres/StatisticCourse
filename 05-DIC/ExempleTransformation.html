3. Data transformation

Which transformation should be used?

When the statistical data comes from counts, it generally follows the Poisson distribution, where the mean and variance are equal. In this case, the square root transformation of the data stabilizes the variance, making it independent of the mean. This transformation can also be used when the variance is proportional to the mean.

n_est_transf <- sqrt(data$n.est + 0.5)
data_tr <- data.frame(coleta = data$coleta, 
                      trat = data$trat,
